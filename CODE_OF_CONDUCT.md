# 🏛️ The Hakaishin Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

However, this repository operates under the **Wave 9 (Sovereign)** standards. We prioritize **Absolute Signal**, **Technical Excellence**, and **Zero Slop**. 

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Focusing on high-density technical signal.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery.
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Submitting low-effort, untested, or "slop" code that wastes maintainer time (Ki).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the repository owner at **Rogercode97**. 

Code or contributions that violate the "Zero Slop" mandate will be rejected (Hakai) without prejudice. The focus is on the code, not the coder.
